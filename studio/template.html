            How to publish a flyer

            From the final PDF to a shoppable flyer on scheels.com. Steps 1 to 4 happen here in Flyer Studio; 5 to 7 in Amplience.

            1. Start the flyer

              Get the final flyer PDF from Creative, and the ad's item list (the marketing status sheet, as .xlsx or .csv) if there is one: Flyer Studio reads its Page, Brand, SKU, Style and In Store Only columns, so hotspots get exact SKUs. Click Open flyer PDF, then Item list. Flyer Studio finds the columns it needs by name; when it can't, it shows the Item list columns window, where Claude suggests them and asks about anything unclear, and Generate stays off until you confirm them. Columns reopens it. With the Claude IBMi Developer Tools connector you can skip the sheet: Load AS400 ad reads the ad's items, prices, pages and SKUs straight from the AS400 (several ad numbers for a flyer that covers more than one ad). An ad set up early may not have its SKUs yet; load it again later to pick them up. Fill in the title, the sale dates and a campaign code: lowercase letters and numbers, like biggame26. The campaign code names the page images and tags the flyer's analytics, so keep it the same from here to Amplience.

            2. Generate the hotspots

              Enter the pages to generate (all, or ranges like 2-10, 26) and click Generate. Claude takes 30 to 60 seconds a page. You can stop and continue later: your work is kept in this browser for this PDF, so reopen the same file to pick up where you left off.

            3. Check every page

              Start with pages marked to check or Failed. When a flagged box is actually right, tick Reviewed in its panel to clear the flag. One flag stays even then: a box whose typed beans leave out a bean its item-list lines have (a second product found later, or a bean deleted by mistake). Add it, or Leave out when the box shows only some of the line's products. Click a box to select it, drag to move it, drag any corner to resize it, click its × (or press Delete) to remove it, or use Draw box to add one: a drawn box fills itself in from the text inside it (Fill from this area does the same for any box). Fix the product name and price on the right; under Item list suggestions, Add each sheet item the box advertises (several when one photo shows several products). Match from item list links every clear match on the page at once. A line marked All whose sheet rows also list particular products (a "Golf Clothing, all styles" line listing the Highlands quarter-zip) links a box that names one of those products to just that product. A box should cover the product's photo, name and price. Pages with no products, like articles or partner logos, should have no boxes. Anything you edit is kept if you generate the page again.

            4. Save the files

              Click Save images + flyer data (.zip) and unzip it. It holds one image per page (flyer-biggame26-p01.jpg and so on) and the flyer data (flyer-biggame26.json). Don't rename the images: Amplience finds each page's image by its name.

            5. Upload the images to Amplience

              In Content Hub, open ECOM Creative › Content Pages › Digital Flyers, upload all the page images and publish them. Unpublished pages stay hidden on the site.

            6. Import into the Digital Flyer item

              Create (or open) a Digital Flyer content item. In its Pages field, choose the .json file. The importer then:

              • shows the title, campaign code and dates to copy into the fields above it;
              • keeps boxes for a whole range (an item-list line marked All) linked to their listing, and changes brand names in links to Scheels' spelling;
              • searches Scheels for every other product and ticks the ones whose brand, model and printed price match. Click Show only ones to check and tick the right product for the rest; leave all unticked to use a search link;
              • checks that each page image is published.

              Click Import pages into this item, then Save the item.

            7. Preview and schedule

              Preview the item and click a few hotspots: matched products open a product panel with price and add to cart. Then publish it, scheduled for the sale dates.

            Working on a flyer together

            Open the PDF, then Shared flyers › Share the open flyer with your team. Sharing uploads the pages, your boxes and the item list, and needs Editor access to Flyer Studio; teammates with Contributor access then open it from Shared flyers, without the PDF. Everyone sees the same boxes, and edits show up for the others as they are made (two people changing the same box: the last change wins). The page list shows who is on which page, and a page someone is generating is skipped by everyone else's Generate. The status beside the buttons says when everything is saved. When Flyer Studio is updated, a window opened before the update turns view-only once someone opens the flyer in the new version; reload it to keep working.

            Who changed what

            A shared flyer keeps an edit log: History beside the buttons lists each change, newest first, with who made it and the fields before and after, and History of this box in a box's panel shows just that box. Shared flyers has a History for every flyer, including deleted ones, with who deleted them. The log is kept until two weeks after the sale ends. Work done before a flyer is shared isn't logged.

            Fixing a live flyer

            Open the Digital Flyer item: the Pages field shows the saved flyer. Move or resize boxes, change a hotspot's products with Find product, or draw and delete boxes, then save the item. Its Page panel adds, replaces and deletes pages, from an image in Content Hub or a page of a Flyer Studio export.

            When Creative sends a revised PDF

            If hotspots were fixed in Amplience since the flyer was imported, first click Export for Flyer Studio in the item, then Load hotspots from Amplience here, so those fixes carry over. Then click Update from revised PDF. Flyer Studio compares the two PDFs page by page: unchanged pages keep their hotspots, each changed page keeps its hotspots (marked to check) or starts over as you choose, new pages are added and pages no longer in the PDF are removed. Pages keep their image names and new pictures get new ones (flyer-biggame26-p12-v2), so nothing already published is overwritten. Generate the pages it fills in under Pages to generate, click Save new page images, upload and publish just those, and import the flyer data into the item with Replace with a new Flyer Studio export. Delete page removes a single page.
